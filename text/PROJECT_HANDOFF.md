# Project Handoff — Take the Streets Back

## Overview
This project is a moody, grounded music video treatment for a Seattle aftermath rap track. The visual approach is atmospheric and realistic, with rainy city textures, implied emergency lighting, and a key Ballard Bridge / school bus / rearview mirror beat.

## Notes for Handoff
- The creative direction is intentionally not sensational — avoid explicit police imagery.
- The key motif is the dog tags in the rearview mirror and the stopped bus at the drawbridge.
- Keep the color story anchored in rain, deep blues, warm red warning light accents, and filmic realism.
