# Project: "Take the Streets Back" — Free Toolchain & AI Producer Guide

## Recommended Free Software Toolchain (Local Setup)
* **Video & Audio Assembly:** **Shotcut** or **DaVinci Resolve (Free Tier)** on Linux Mint. Used for multi-track timeline editing, audio syncing, pacing cuts, and applying color grading.
* **Audio Mastering & Mixing:** **Audacity** (Open-source) for fine-tuning vocal tracks, adjusting compression, and balancing the 85 BPM beat against the rap flow and piano bridge.
* **Asset & Prompt Management:** Local text editor (VS Code) for keeping this README and your prompt lists organized.

---

## Free AI Image & Video Generators (Zero-Cost Tier)
To generate the landmark montage, atmospheric light washes, and specific cinematic frames without spending a dime, utilize these free-tier platforms:

### 1. **Google Gemini (Built-in)**
* **What it’s good for:** Generating high-resolution, photorealistic stills of the landmarks (Space Needle, Great Wheel, Ballard Bridge, Fremont Troll, Hammering Man, Pike Place Market, Central Library) and abstract emergency lighting effects right here in the chat interface.
* **Cost:** Free.

### 2. **Luma Dream Machine (Free Tier)**
* **What it’s good for:** Taking your generated landmark or abstract stills and turning them into subtle, cinematic 3D motion clips (camera pans, slow zooms, rain movement).
* **Cost:** Offers free generational credits upon signup.

### 3. **Kling AI or Runway Gen-3 (Free Trials/Tiers)**
* **What it’s good for:** High-end motion text-to-video capabilities. Great for generating short loops of rain-slicked pavement with flashing red and blue light sweeps or the static bridge atmosphere.
* **Cost:** Free tier/starter credits available.

### 4. **Pexels & Pixabay (Free Stock Footage)**
* **What it’s good for:** Sourcing raw B-roll elements that are harder to prompt, like general city drizzle, passing traffic lights, or ambient street textures.
* **Cost:** 100% free, royalty-free stock video and images.
