# Take the Streets Back — Prompt README

This prompt pack is designed for short-form AI video generation and image-to-video workflows. The focus is on a moody, grounded, cinematic look for a dark Seattle aftermath music video.

## Style Notes
Use these as recurring modifiers across prompts:
- cinematic
- moody and atmospheric
- heavy rain
- wet reflective surfaces
- dark realism
- subtle emergency lighting
- grounded emotional tone
- photorealistic
- 4K

## Negative Prompt
Avoid cartoonish look, exaggerated horror, bright neon overload, fantasy elements, low realism, warped geometry, glitchy motion, overexposed lighting, and overly dramatic lens flare.

---

## Seedance Final Prompt
Use this exact prompt and negative prompt for the rearview mirror / bus / dog tags shot.

Prompt:
"Rearview mirror close-up inside a worn car stopped on the Ballard Bridge at night, waiting for the drawbridge to go up or down; raindrops and streaks on the windshield distort a dark Seattle sky; a yellow school bus is visible ahead through the wet glass, only its slow red warning lights glowing; a pair of tarnished military dog tags dangle from the mirror, swinging gently with subtle camera parallax; soft, implied reflections of red/amber emergency light washes on wet glass and chrome (do not show any police vehicles or uniforms); ultra-realistic photographic render, cinematic film stock look, shallow depth of field, soft bokeh, tactile surface detail (water beads, grime, worn metal), muted palette with deep blues and warm red accents, low-key lighting, gentle handheld camera push-in, subtle motion blur, natural skin/textures, restrained and intimate mood, photoreal, avoid stylized or painterly effects — output for an 8s clip."

Negative prompt:
"Avoid bright daylight, police cars or officers, uniforms, badges, explicit gore, exaggerated neon, cartoonish textures, heavy lens flare, obvious CGI artifacts, painterly or anime styles."

Recommended Seedance settings:
- Duration: 8s
- FPS: 16
- Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (or 16:9)
- Guidance / CFG: 6.5–8.0
- Steps: 30–40
- Image-reference weight: 0.6–0.8
- Motion strength: 0.18–0.30
- Variants: 3
